# TimeLedge Privacy Policy

Effective August 31, 2026

TimeLedge does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The app has
no networking, analytics, advertising, telemetry, or account system.

TimeLedge stores clock format, appearance, display, visibility, and launch-at-
login preferences locally in the app's UserDefaults domain. Those settings
remain on the Mac and are not sent off-device.

TimeLedge does not request Accessibility, Screen Recording, camera, microphone,
location, contacts, or calendar permission. Its fullscreen visibility logic
reads public window metadata and does not capture screen pixels or window
content.

For privacy questions or support, open an issue in the
[TimeLedge GitHub repository](https://github.com/miles-chu10/TimeLedge/issues).

Material changes to this policy will be published in this file with an updated
effective date.
